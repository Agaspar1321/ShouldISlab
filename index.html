Know before you slab it.

Most grading calculators show what you'd make if the card comes back a 10. This one weighs every grade by how often it actually happens, using real PSA submission counts, then takes off grading and selling fees. You get your expected profit, the grade you need to break even, and a straight answer when the smart move is not to grade.

    here's the catch

    The "if it gems" number is mostly fiction.

    On a 1999 Base Charizard, about 0.5% of PSA submissions come back a 10 and 58% grade 6 or below. A calculator that assumes the 10 is pricing a 1-in-200 shot as a sure thing.

    Typical calculator 0.5%

    Prices your card as if it comes back a PSA 10 — which happens about once in every 200 submissions.

    ShouldISlab 58%

    Counts the copies that grade 6 or below too, valued at raw — then weighs every grade by how often it really happens.
