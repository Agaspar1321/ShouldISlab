Know before you slab it.

Every other calculator shows you the dream — what you'd make if it gems a 10. ShouldISlab weighs that against the real odds, taken from actual PSA submission counts, not a number someone guessed. It prices every grade from recent sales, subtracts grading and selling fees, and tells you your true expected profit, your break-even grade, and when the smart move is to not grade at all.
